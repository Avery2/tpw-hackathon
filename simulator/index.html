The part no architecture fixes

Program cycles in the data end between January and June 2026. Under CDCR's primary measure — 3-year conviction rate — the first cohort that can be read at all matures in 2029.

Read this honestly. Every number above assumes each completer is tokenised, released, matched and poolable — across 36 cycles, 14 institutions, and two organisations with analysis-ready rows and different definitions of "completed". Today, zero of those cycles has a mature 3-year window, and the aggregate design would need a 200-point effect to fire — a larger reduction than the gap between full recidivism (40.6%) and none at all. The simulator shows a limitation, and that limitation is the ask.
